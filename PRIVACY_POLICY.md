# Privacy Policy for Global Minds School

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

**Global Minds School** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Global Minds School** mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the personal privacy and data safety of students, parents, guardians, teachers, and staff members who use our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our services, and describes your rights under applicable data protection and children's online privacy regulations (including COPPA, FERPA, and Google Play Families Policies).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided Directly by the School / Users
- **Account & Profile Information:** Student/Parent/Staff full names, admission numbers/student IDs, assigned grades/classes, email addresses, phone numbers, and profile photos.
- **Academic & School Records:** Attendance records, class timetables, report cards/exam results, homework assignments, teacher feedback, and school fee status.
- **Communication & Notices:** Circulars, announcements, event calendars, and school-to-parent messages.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & Notification Tokens:** Device push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Expo Push Tokens) to deliver critical real-time alerts, circulars, and attendance updates.
- **App Usage & Diagnostic Information:** App crash logs, performance diagnostics, and system version (used exclusively for app stability and security).

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

The App only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver school services:

| Permission | Purpose | Why Google Play Requires It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` | Communicates securely with school servers and cloud database. | Essential for fetching attendance, timetables, circulars, and student records. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Delivers real-time push notifications (Android 13+). | Required to receive emergency notices, school alerts, daily attendance, and circulars. |
| `VIBRATE` | Provides haptic feedback when urgent alerts or notifications arrive. | Enhances user experience for important messages. |

> **Note:** The App does **NOT** access your precise location, contacts, microphones, camera, or shared external storage unless explicitly requested with runtime user consent.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for educational and administrative purposes:
1. To facilitate seamless communication between the school, teachers, students, and parents.
2. To provide real-time updates regarding student attendance, test results, fee receipts, and homework.
3. To deliver critical announcements and emergency alerts via push notifications.
4. To maintain secure authentication and protect student records from unauthorized access.
5. We **DO NOT** sell, rent, or monetize personal student or parent data under any circumstances.
6. We **DO NOT** serve third-party behavioral advertisements.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is our top priority.
- All student accounts are provisioned, authorized, and managed by the school administration or directly supervised by verified parents/guardians.
- The App complies with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, **Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)**, and **Google Play Developer Policy on Families**.
- We never collect personal data from minors for commercial or advertising purposes.
- Parents/Guardians have the right to review their child’s records or request account updates through the school administration.

---

## 5. Data Security and Protection

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications between the mobile app and backend servers utilize industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Only authorized parents, verified guardians, teachers, and school administrators can access specific student records.
- **Secure Cloud Infrastructure:** Data is securely hosted with reputable enterprise cloud providers adhering to strict security standards.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- We retain educational records as required by school policy and educational regulatory standards.
- **Account Deletion & Data Removal:** In compliance with Google Play Store Account Deletion requirements, users or parents wishing to delete their account or request removal of their personal data can submit a request directly through the app or by emailing our data protection officer at:
  - **Email:** `support@globalmindsschool.edu` (or `admin@tplpro.com`)
  - Upon verification, the requested account access and associated mobile tokens will be permanently removed.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our services or changes in legal regulations. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date and posting a notice within the App.
