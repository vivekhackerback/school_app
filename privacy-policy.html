Privacy Policy

Global Minds School • Last updated: September 23, 2026

Global Minds School ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Global Minds School mobile application (the "App"). We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and confidence of our students, parents, guardians, teachers, and school personnel.

Commitment to Privacy: We strictly adhere to student privacy regulations including COPPA, FERPA, and Google Play Store Developer Policies. We never sell, monetize, or share student data with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver educational and school management services:

  • User & Student Profile Data: Full names, admission numbers, student IDs, class/grade, verified parent contact details (phone, email), and profile photos.
  • Academic & School Records: Attendance logs, daily timetables, homework assignments, marks, teacher evaluations, and fee payment status.
  • Notification Identifiers: Device push tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Expo Push Service) used exclusively to dispatch real-time school circulars, attendance alerts, and emergency notifications.
  • Application Diagnostics: Crash logs and app performance data used strictly to resolve technical issues and maintain app stability.

2. App Permissions & Play Store Compliance

Our app adheres to the principle of least privilege, requesting only required operational permissions:

Permission Type Purpose
INTERNET Normal Allows the app to fetch school circulars, student attendance, timetables, and academic updates securely via HTTPS.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Enables real-time push alerts for important school announcements, emergency updates, and attendance alerts.
VIBRATE Normal Provides haptic vibration for important school notifications.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Because our app is used within an educational institution serving children, we maintain strict privacy safeguards:

  • Student accounts are created and supervised under institutional school authority or directly by parents/guardians.
  • No behavioral profiling, ad targeting, or third-party data tracking is conducted within the application.
  • Parents and legal guardians may request review or correction of their child's educational records at any time by contacting the school office.

4. Data Security & Storage

We enforce stringent safeguards including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), role-based access restrictions, and secure cloud infrastructure to ensure that student records remain confidential and protected against unauthorized access.
